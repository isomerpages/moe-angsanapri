<p><strong>Applied Learning Programme (ALP)</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Developing&nbsp;21st Century Competencies Through Design Thinking for Sustainability</strong>
</p>
<p>At Angsana Primary School, sustainability education is more than just
learning about the environment. It is a way for students to build the essential
skills they need for the future.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Collaboration: Students work together on real‑world environmental challenges,
learning to listen to different perspectives and communicate effectively
to reach shared solutions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Critical Thinking: They examine how environmental systems work, weigh
evidence, and consider different approaches to sustainability.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Creativity and Adaptability: Students design innovative solutions, from
new ideas for reducing waste to creative ways of conserving resources.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Resilience and Confidence: In an assessment‑free environment, students
are encouraged to think boldly and explore new ideas without fear of failure.
This helps them grow more adaptable, confident, and ready to face future
challenges.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Global Literacy: Students become aware of sustainability issues around
the world, such as climate change, resource use, and waste management.
They learn to see connections between local actions and global impact and
understand their role as responsible citizens in a shared world.</p>
<p><strong>Recycling Efforts at Angsana</strong>
</p>
<p>At Angsana Primary School, sustainability comes alive through our school
wide recycling programme. Led by our Eco Stewards, all students take part
in meaningful recycling activities that connect classroom learning to everyday
life.</p>
<p>What Students Do</p>
<p>• Learn from peers: Eco Stewards guide classmates on how to sort and dispose
of materials correctly and share the importance of recycling through informal
conversations.</p>
<p>• Bring recyclables from home: Students contribute items to the school’s
recycling drive, reinforcing responsibility and care for the environment.</p>
<p>• Apply classroom learning: Recycling activities make sustainability lessons
tangible and practical.</p>
<p>Our efforts are strengthened through collaboration with CORA Environment,
a waste management company that supports the school in implementing recycling
solutions. By making recycling part of daily school life, Angsana nurtures
students who are not only environmentally aware but also confident, responsible,
and ready to contribute to a greener future</p>
