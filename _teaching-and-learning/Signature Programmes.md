<p><strong>Applied Learning Programme (ALP)</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Developing&nbsp;21st Century Competencies Through Design Thinking for Sustainability</strong>
</p>
<p>At Angsana Primary School, sustainability education is more than just
learning about the environment. It is a way for students to build the essential
skills they need for the future.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Collaboration: Students work together on real‑world environmental challenges,
learning to listen to different perspectives and communicate effectively
to reach shared solutions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Critical Thinking: They examine how environmental systems work, weigh
evidence, and consider different approaches to sustainability.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Creativity and Adaptability: Students design innovative solutions, from
new ideas for reducing waste to creative ways of conserving resources.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Resilience and Confidence: In an assessment‑free environment, students
are encouraged to think boldly and explore new ideas without fear of failure.
This helps them grow more adaptable, confident, and ready to face future
challenges.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Global Literacy: Students become aware of sustainability issues around
the world, such as climate change, resource use, and waste management.
They learn to see connections between local actions and global impact and
understand their role as responsible citizens in a shared world.</p>
<p><strong>Recycling Efforts at Angsana</strong>
</p>
<p>At Angsana Primary School, sustainability comes alive through our school
wide recycling programme. Led by our Eco Stewards, all students take part
in meaningful recycling activities that connect classroom learning to everyday
life.</p>
<p>What Students Do</p>
<p>• Learn from peers: Eco Stewards guide classmates on how to sort and dispose
of materials correctly and share the importance of recycling through informal
conversations.</p>
<p>• Bring recyclables from home: Students contribute items to the school’s
recycling drive, reinforcing responsibility and care for the environment.</p>
<p>• Apply classroom learning: Recycling activities make sustainability lessons
tangible and practical.</p>
<p>Our efforts are strengthened through collaboration with CORA Environment,
a waste management company that supports the school in implementing recycling
solutions. By making recycling part of daily school life, Angsana nurtures
students who are not only environmentally aware but also confident, responsible,
and ready to contribute to a greener future</p>

<p><strong>Eco Spotlight by Eco Stewards</strong>
</p>
<p>At Angsana Primary School, our Eco Stewards are given opportunities to
go beyond recycling and sustainability week activities. Through the Eco
Spotlight, they advocate for environmental issues and inspire their peers
to take action.</p>
<p>What Students Do</p>
<p>• Present during assemblies: Eco Stewards prepare short talks, skits,
or multimedia presentations to share practical tips such as reducing single
use plastics, conserving water, and recycling.</p>
<p>• Teach and inspire peers: By sharing their knowledge and passion, they
encourage classmates to adopt sustainable habits in fun and relatable ways.</p>
<p>Eco Spotlight shows students that advocacy can be powerful and joyful.
By leading their peers, Eco Stewards learn that small actions such as conserving
water or reducing plastic use. This can create meaningful change in the
school community and beyond.</p>

<p><strong>Learning for Life Programme (LLP)</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Nurturing and Developing 21st Century Leaders through Enrichment and Lifeskills (ENL) Programmes</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Developing Student Leadership Through Sustainability and Beyond</strong>
</p>
<p>At Angsana Primary School, we believe every child has the potential to
be a leader. Through our Learning for Life Programme (LLP), students are
nurtured as active change agents, taking ownership of real initiatives
that make a difference in their school and community. Guided by our school
vision — A Community of Curious Minds and Serving Hearts — we provide opportunities
for students to grow as leaders, build strong relationships, and learn
how to work effectively in teams.</p>
<p><strong>Leadership Through Sustainability</strong>
</p>
<p>Our Eco Stewards and student leaders play key roles in driving sustainability
efforts. They:</p>
<p>• Plan and lead school wide campaigns.</p>
<p>• Educate peers about eco friendly practices.</p>
<p>• Advocate for positive change in fun and meaningful ways.</p>
<p>These authentic roles help students build confidence, communication skills,
and project management abilities. By taking responsibility for environmental
outcomes, they develop a service mindset and see their power to create
real impact.</p>
<p><strong>Leadership Beyond Sustainability</strong>
</p>
<p>While sustainability is a key focus, LLP also develops student leaders
through Leader in Me platform and in these five key areas:</p>
<p>• Citizenship – learning to contribute responsibly to society.</p>
<p>• Self Management and Social Skills – building resilience, empathy, and
teamwork.</p>
<p>• Physical Activities and Sports – fostering discipline, teamwork, and
perseverance.</p>
<p>• Aesthetics – expressing creativity and confidence through the arts.</p>
<p>• Enterprise – developing initiative, innovation, and problem solving
skills.</p>
<p>Through these experiences, our students learn to influence others, manage
projects, and communicate effectively with diverse groups. They grow into
confident leaders who care for the environment, their peers and the wider
community.</p>
<p><strong>The Leader-in-Me Programme </strong>
</p>
<p>We aim for every student to:</p>
<p>• Attain Personal Mastery – develop good habits in self management.</p>
<p>• Work in Teams – learn how to collaborate and support one another.</p>
<p>• Lead Others – acquire the skills to guide and inspire their peers.</p>
<p>To nurture these qualities, we use the Leader in Me framework, based on
the 7 Habits of Happy Kids:</p>
<p>• Habits 1–3 and 7 focus on self leadership and independence.</p>
<p>• Habits 4–6 focus on teamwork and learning to work well with others.</p>
<p>Form Teachers conduct Leader in Me lessons for Primary 1 to 4 students
(2–3 hours each term), focusing on character development and leadership
habits. For Primary 5 and 6, students reflect on their leadership journey
using curated resources on the Student Learning Space (SLS).</p>
<p>The skills cultivated through the Leader in Me programme — such as self
management, teamwork, and leading others — are reinforced in:</p>
<p>• The LIGHT Programme, where students apply leadership in sustainability
projects.</p>
<p>• The Train the Trainer workshops, where selected students guide their
peers and take on mentoring roles.</p>
<p>Through these experiences, our students learn that leadership is not just
about leading others, but also about serving the community and making a
positive impact.</p>
<p><strong>The Student Leadership Challenge Workshop with Design Thinking</strong>
</p>
<p>Selected student leaders took part in hands on workshops designed to strengthen
their leadership skills while learning the Design Thinking process. Through
engaging activities, they explored real experiences in the school community
and turned these insights into meaningful solutions.</p>
<p>For the Primary 3–4 leaders, students observed everyday school spaces
such as classrooms and the canteen, spoke with peers, and designed ideas
to improve these spaces. For the Primary 5–6 cohort, students interviewed
teachers to understand their daily experiences and created thoughtful concepts
for Teachers’ Day appreciation activities.</p>
<p>Applying their learning, students worked in teams to brainstorm, prototype,
and present their ideas. This experience encouraged them to think critically,
collaborate effectively, and communicate confidently, while building empathy
and creativity.</p>
